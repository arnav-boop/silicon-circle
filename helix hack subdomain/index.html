01 / About HelixHack

What is HelixHack?

HelixHack is a structured, mentored, and genuinely rigorous competition designed specifically for school-age innovators. Held in Chennai, it challenges teams to develop projects within Pitch-Only (ideation-focused) or Prototype (working software/hardware) categories.

Silicon Circle is a teen-led community. We believe students do not need to wait until they graduate before making meaningful contributions to real-world problems. This hackathon is the platform we wish had existed when we started.

Event Format Qualifying + Offline Finale
Location Chennai, India
Eligibility Grades 8 to 12
Team Size 2 to 5 Members

02 / Core Focus Areas

Hackathon Themes

All projects must address a problem in one of these four broad domains.

🤖

AI & Emerging Tech

Projects exploring AI, machine learning, computer vision, or robotics to automate and solve practical, real-world issues.

E.g. Tamil language AI assistants, automated sorting.
🌱

Sustainability & Climate

Environmental preservation, renewable energy, climate resilience, conservation, or waste optimization systems.

E.g. Rainwater monitoring, carbon tracking.
🧠

Wellbeing & EdTech

Tackling student mental health challenges, emotional wellbeing, stress support systems, and learning platform innovations.

E.g. Peer-support forums, study schedulers.
♿

Accessibility & Inclusion

Removing learning, economic, linguistic, and physical barriers to ensure full societal participation for everyone.

E.g. Assistive devices, sign translators.

05 / Support

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the registration, evaluation, and policies.

Students currently enrolled in Grade 8 through Grade 12 in Chennai schools. Independent teens not enrolled in a school are also eligible to register. All teams must have 2 to 5 members.
AI tools (Copilot, Cursor, ChatGPT, etc.) are allowed for coding, refactoring, asset building, and research. However, AI is NOT permitted to write your core innovation or problem statements. Additionally, every team member must be able to thoroughly explain all code and design decisions during judging.
Judging is worth 100 points: Innovation & Originality (25), STEM Integration (20), Feasibility (20), Impact (15), Execution Quality (10), and Presentation (10) + a 5-point bonus for consistent daily Discord logging.
Registration and Round 1 qualifying are completely free. If your team advances to the offline finale, a registration fee is required to cover venue entry, lunch, and amenities.
